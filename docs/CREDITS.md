## This codebase
- Written as an original implementation of the Devouring Storms concepts.
- All textures are generated by `tools/generate_assets.py` (pure mathematics, no source art).
- All sounds/music are synthesized by `tools/generate_assets.py` (pure mathematics, no samples).
- All structures (Endertown, the Watcher Shrine, the Mainframe Ruin, the Rift Obelisks) are
  original layouts generated block-by-block by `tools/generate_structures.py` — inspired by the
  *idea* of Endertown from the public DR V2 wiki, not copied from its actual builds or assets.
- The *We Have Been Changed* and *Ships to Carry Us Home* discs are chiptune interpretations built
  note-by-note from the trailer's vocal themes, not recordings of the trailer audio.
