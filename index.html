LOW · Realtime Simulation Suite

Two Worlds. One Engine.

Two standalone realtime simulations built on Babylon.js (WebGL 2, HDR pipeline, GPU ray-marched optics). Click a simulation to launch it. Best experienced in a Chromium browser with hardware acceleration enabled.

🌊 Ocean Worlds — Water Physics Simulator

A playable water playground: grab, place and throw floating props with the mouse, summon whirlpools, flood or drain the sea, call down rain and thunder, sail a cloth-bannered raft, ride currents — on eight handcrafted planets, from lava tides to bioluminescent nights. Free-fly camera included.

🕳️ Singularity Vault — Black Hole Customizer

Take the black hole itself by the mouse and drag it through space. Fly a free camera straight into the horizon. Summon a sun, gas giants, an asteroid belt, a comet — even a second black hole. Then descend: the Bloodstream, the Hollow House, the Kaleidoscope Void, and a new forged singularity every loop. 24 curated presets + 4 billion seeded forges.
